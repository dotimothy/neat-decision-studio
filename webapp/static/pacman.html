Space or Start Race starts two games in the same maze, with the same ghosts. Against you, steer yours with the arrow keys or WASD; it moves eight squares a second, as Laya's does at watch speed, and turns at the next opening the way you last pressed. Against another loaded model, that model is asked what Laya is asked, and they race again when both are done.

Scores are kept until reset.

What It Is Told

–

The model is not shown the maze. The harness works out where each open way leads and says it in a few words; those descriptions are the options of the question. It tries each step to see whether a ghost would catch Pac-Man, calls a way dangerous when it closes in on a ghost four squares away or nearer, and otherwise says whether it brings Pac-Man closer to the food: a blue ghost it can still catch, or else the nearest dot.
